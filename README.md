# Become a Co-founder — Palo Alto AI Research Lab

**Live page: [palo-alto-ai-research-lab.github.io/cofounder](https://palo-alto-ai-research-lab.github.io/cofounder/)**

We are building an AI company in public: one founder, a fleet of autonomous AI
agents, and a network built since 2015 across AI, Web3 and VC. The engine runs
daily. We are opening a few co-founder seats for people who want to build rather
than watch.

This repository holds the open call itself — a single self-contained
[`index.html`](index.html) page with the application form. No tracking scripts,
no build step, no dependencies: open the file and it works.

## This is for you if

- You are obsessed with what AI agents can actually do — the real leverage, not the hype.
- You bring a superpower we do not have yet: capital, a network, distribution, deep tech, or a market you own.
- You would rather own a piece of something wild than rent a title at something safe.
- Building in public excites you rather than scares you.

## This is not for you if

- You are looking for a salaried job with a fixed scope.
- You want a passive "advisor" badge for your bio.
- Your idea of commitment is "let's grab coffee sometime."
- You need someone else to have already de-risked it.

## How to apply
